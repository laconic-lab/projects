Environment: localai-box (Ubuntu 24.04) on Steam Deck SteamOS.

Objective and diagnostic context:
- Investigated incomplete long answers produced by Shyrka.
- An earlier response was found to end mid-sentence in the archived
  assistant message itself. This established that the stored answer was
  incomplete; it did not, by itself, identify the underlying cause.

  window, while num_predict limits generated output tokens.

- Resulting num_predict: 4096.
- num_ctx: 4096 before and after the change.

  were unchanged at documentation time.
- The profile was read back and the resulting values were confirmed.
