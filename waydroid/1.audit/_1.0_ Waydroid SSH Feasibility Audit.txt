3. The ioctl investigation — resolved
I checked the upstream Linux BinderFS UAPI definition. We have found the problem with our previous
device-creation test.
The actual definition is:
BINDER_CTL_ADD is _IOWR('b', 1, struct binderfs_device) .
The structure contains a 256-byte name, followed by two 32-bit fields: major and minor .
Its total size is 264 bytes.
The request therefore needs to encode a read/write operation and the full 264-byte structure.
GitHub +1
Our previous request, 0x40046201 , encoded a different ioctl operation and an incorrect structure size.
That explains why the kernel returned EINVAL . The previous failure was a test-construction error, not
evidence that Podman cannot create Binder devices.
